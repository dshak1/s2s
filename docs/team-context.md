## The Maze failure, which matters

> "It was very hard for parents to help their children connect to the platform
> while being on Zoom. Now the plan is to gather everyone in person again."

A research session was effectively lost to **onboarding friction**. Parents could
not get kids into the platform over a video call.

This is the strongest single piece of evidence in the whole export, and it
validates the zero-login design: no account, no password, no email. It also says
the remaining friction (a session code, a device that forgets you) is not a
small thing. It is the difference between getting data and not getting data.

Maze test link: `https://t.maze.co/560682370`

   has set with the group.
